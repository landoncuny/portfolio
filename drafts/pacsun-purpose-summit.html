Project 3: Pacsun Purpose Summit

Challenge

The Pacsun Purpose Summit gathers 300+ leaders and creators across fashion, media, wellness, sports, and entertainment for a day of programming built around the annual Youth Report by Pacsun — grounded in insights from 6,000+ Gen Z and Gen Alpha voices. Returning on September 24, 2026, the summit will unpack the new report and its findings (including AI) across thought-provoking panels, immersive brand activations, and networking, while opening the day to partners for the first time.

Building the Purpose Summit

Process

1. Research

I performed site visits to multiple venues, partnering with management for LACMA, the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures, and Rolling Green. I also researched brands that could potentially represent the AI moment and developed parameters and selections for the Youth 25 List.

2. Coordination

I helped coordinate the day's programming — panels, interactive activations, and networking moments. I aligned speakers, schedules, and logistics across teams. I also coordinated catering details, including a breakfast, lunch, and cocktail bar, with the venue and the business partners in attendance.

3. Reachout

I supported outreach to partners and creators, walking them through partnership tiers — from Supporting and Moment levels up to Featured, Platform, and Presenting — to secure sponsorships, activation footprints, and main-stage moments.

4. Execution

On the day, I will help bring the summit to life — supporting panel transitions, partner activations, the step-and-repeat photo moment, and gift-bag logistics to keep the experience seamless for leaders and creators.

What the Summit Will Deliver

Goals

The Pacsun Purpose Summit will bring 300+ industry leaders and creators together to release the 2026 Youth Report and explore what its findings mean for the next generation. Through panels, immersive brand activations, and partner engagement, the summit will reinforce Pacsun's purpose-driven mission and give partners a new way to connect with the voices shaping youth culture.
